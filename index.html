    Quantum states of light in cavity QED: A computational study of Wigner function dynamics and atom–field entanglement in the Jaynes–Cummings model

    NK Nguyen. Advised by Luca Dal Negro.

    Preprint · Under review at the European Journal of Physics · 2026

    PDF ResearchGate Code Zenodo

    A numerical study of the resonant Jaynes–Cummings model (n̄ = 10). Time-resolved Wigner functions show the coherent field becoming a Schrödinger-cat-like state at half the revival time (Wigner negativity δ = 0.85, purity 0.96), where the atom and field nearly disentangle; coherent, thermal, squeezed-vacuum and Fock fields are compared through the reduced atomic entropy and the logarithmic negativity.

    Engineering Fourier Optics

    NK Nguyen.

    Based on lectures by Luca Dal Negro.

    268 pages · Third draft · Unfinished

    Read Errata

    A textbook draft on Fourier optics for engineers: linear systems, the angular spectrum, Fresnel and Fraunhofer diffraction, and numerical propagation, with Python and MATLAB code for every section.

    Also covers sampling, arrays, gratings, Talbot self-imaging, zone plates and scalar diffraction theory.

    Part of Engineering Fourier Optics: Theory, Applications and Computation, a draft soon to be submitted to Springer. All text and figures are my own. The draft began as my notes on Luca Dal Negro's EC 562 Fourier Optics in Engineering (Fall 2023) and Lei Tian's EC 522 Computational Optical Imaging (Spring 2024) at Boston University, and has grown well beyond them, with derivations, worked examples, and Python and MATLAB code for each section. Further application chapters, written by senior researchers and PhD students, are in progress and not yet included.
