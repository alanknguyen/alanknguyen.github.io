I am a master's student in Electrical and Computer Engineering (Systems Robotics) at Boston University, where I work on embodied AI in the Dependable Computing Lab. My current work is a perception-to-action stack for a 7-DoF Sawyer arm: vision-language models for grounding, ROS 2 for execution, and Isaac Sim for sim-to-real transfer.

Before this I completed a B.A. in Physics and Computer Science at BU (2025), with research in computational optics: quantum states of light in cavity QED with Luca Dal Negro, intensity diffraction tomography with Lei Tian, and finite-element simulation of metasurfaces. I co-founded the BU AI Society and taught its Systems-First Machine Vision course.

This site collects my reports, course notes, and paper reviews. Project write-ups will follow.
