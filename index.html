I'm a junior research engineer working on robot learning, robot perception, and machine vision systems. I'm at Boston University, advised by Wenchao Li in the Dependable Computing Lab and co-advised by Eshed Ohn-Bar in the H2X Lab. I'm most interested in VLMs and VLAs and how to put them to work in robotics, and in physics-informed approaches to ML and RL.

I graduated from BU in 2025 with dual degrees in Physics and Computer Science. As an undergraduate I was lucky to be advised by Lei Tian in computational imaging and Luca Dal Negro in nanophotonics. I've been an intern at Scale and Leuko, and I co-founded and led the BU AI Society.

    NK Nguyen. Advised by Luca Dal Negro.

    Preprint · Under review at the European Journal of Physics · 2026

    PDF ResearchGate Code Zenodo

  1. Fourier Optics: Graduate Lecture Notes

    NK Nguyen. Based on lectures by Luca Dal Negro.

    Main source: BU ENG EC 562, Fourier Optics in Engineering

    Read

    Draft
    Version 3 (finished)

    Part of Elements of Fourier Optics: A Computational Approach, submitted to Springer. All text and figures are my own. The draft began as my notes on Luca Dal Negro's EC 562 Fourier Optics in Engineering lectures at Boston University (Fall 2023) and has grown well beyond them, with derivations, worked examples and extensive Python code.

    Linear systems, the angular spectrum and free-space propagation, Fresnel and Fraunhofer diffraction, sampling and numerical propagation, arrays, gratings, Talbot self-imaging, zone plates and scalar diffraction theory, with extensive Python code.
