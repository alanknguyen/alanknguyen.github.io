I'm a junior research engineer working on robot learning, robot perception, and machine vision systems, with a particular interest in end-to-end learning. I'm at Boston University, advised by Wenchao Li in the Dependable Computing Lab and co-advised by Eshed Ohn-Bar in the H2X Lab. I also build production vision systems, including Crown Barber Studio, a 3D head-scanning iOS app.

I graduated from BU in 2025 with dual degrees in Physics and Computer Science, with a research background in computational imaging, optics, and photonics. I've been an intern at Scale and Leuko, and I co-founded and led the BU AI Society.

Research

  1. Quantum States of Light in Cavity QED: A Computational Study of Wigner Function Dynamics and Atom–Field Entanglement in the Jaynes–Cummings Model

    NK Nguyen. Advised by Luca Dal Negro.

    Preprint · Under review at the European Journal of Physics · 2026

    PDF ResearchGate Code Zenodo

    In the Jaynes–Cummings model, the cavity field forms a nearly pure Schrödinger cat at half the revival time, just as atom and field nearly disentangle. Weak cavity loss destroys most of it.
