    Theory of Optimal and Robust Control

    NK Nguyen, scribe. Lectures by Sean Andersson.

    2025 · 8 notes · 60 pages

    A rigorous introduction to optimal and robust control. Robust control: model reduction, H2 and H∞ control, and feedback control of uncertain systems. Optimal control: the linear quadratic regulator, the calculus of variations, the maximum principle and the Hamilton–Jacobi–Bellman equation.

    Dynamic Programming and Stochastic Control

    NK Nguyen, scribe. Lectures by Michael Caramanis.

    2025 · 23 notes · 104 pages

    The theory of dynamic programming for sequential decisions under uncertainty: the DP algorithm and its optimality conditions, linear-quadratic problems, inventory control and asset selling, imperfect state information, and infinite-horizon problems with approximate DP.
