Research

I work at the intersection of robotics, machine learning, and physics. My current focus is on AI-driven systems robotics — VLM/VLA-based perception-to-action pipelines, reinforcement learning for control, and simulation-to-real transfer. My physics background spans condensed matter, photonics, and computational electromagnetics.

Current

Embodied AI & Systems Robotics

BU Dependable Computing Lab (DCL) & RASTIC · Sep 2025 – Present

Architecting VLM/VLA inference-to-execution pipelines for a 7-DoF Sawyer manipulator. Perception stack with RGB-D, SAM/GroundingDINO, 6-DoF pose estimation. Sim-to-real via IsaacSim. Working on an RL paradigm paper with PhD students.

RL for Robotic Manipulation

BU Dependable Computing Lab · In progress

RL-driven control policies with VLM/LLM/VLA-based perception. Integrating SO101 Robot arm, hexapod SLAM bots, and lightweight quadcopter drones.
